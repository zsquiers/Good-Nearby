Feel-good things to do on the South Shore

From Hingham and Scituate down through Marshfield, Duxbury, Pembroke and Plymouth, the South Shore pairs ocean air with a growing wellness community: JPH at Ferry Hill's mostly free programs in Marshfield, Scituate Salt Cave, restorative yoga series and coastal walks like World's End.
