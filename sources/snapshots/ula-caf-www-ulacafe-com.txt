Ula Cafe is a friendly neighborhood bakery, café and meetup spot
for all of us.
Come join us for some soul-nourishing, wholesome and fresh savory fare, scrumptious pastries, and delectable coffee and tea.
Stay for the comfort of community in a neighborhood that is uniquely ours.
We are committed to our role as a responsible, conscientious and eco-conscious employer, neighbor and community member.
We can’t wait to welcome you!

We live the values we want to see in Boston:
All Inclusive
No matter how you look, who you love, or what you eat, we welcome you. People love our vegan + gluten-free food options too!
Wholesome + Organic
We make almost everything in house, and when possible, keep it as wholesome and organic as we can so you can feed body
and
soul.
Responsible + Fair
From fair trade coffee to hiring and treating people equitably, we take our decisions seriously and make them consciously.
Local + Global
We’re committed to partnering and supporting locally, while celebrating the global diversity represented in our neighborhood.
