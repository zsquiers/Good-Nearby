good questions.
Is bōm dough gluten-free?
+
−
Pão de queijo, mochi waffles and our burritos are naturally gluten-free, and gluten-free options run through the menu. Brioche and bagels share the kitchen, so ask us about cross-contact.
What kinds of dough do you serve?
+
−
Six, from six places: pão de queijo (Brazil), brioche (France), bagels (New York), mochi waffles (Japan), gnocchi (Italy) and gluten-free burritos (Mexico). Baked fresh daily at both Cambridge cafés.
Where are you in Cambridge, and when are you open?
+
−
Inman Square, 1271 Cambridge St, and Lechmere, 1 Canal Park — both open weekdays 8am–3pm, weekends 8am–4pm. Both serve the full menu of dough and coffee.
Do you serve brunch?
+
−
Yes — all day, every day, at both cafés: weekdays 8am–3pm, weekends 8am–4pm. Walk in, no reservations. Gluten-free eggs benedict on warm pão de queijo, mochi waffles, açaí bowls and sandwiches on your choice of dough, with coffee and matcha.
Do you serve lunch?
+
−
Yes — the full menu runs from open to close: sandwiches on your choice of dough, burritos, plates and bowls, soup and sides. Both cafés are open until 3pm on weekdays and 4pm on weekends.
How do I order pickup or delivery?
+
−
Order ahead through the Order button for the fastest pickup. Delivery is available via DoorDash, Uber Eats and Toast.
How do rewards work?
+
−
Every visit counts — any order of $5 or more. Give us your phone number at the register or at checkout online; after six visits your next drink is on us — coffee, latte, matcha, lemonade or tea. Add your birthday for a treat that month.
Do you cater?
+
−
Yes — breakfast for offices, teams and events across Cambridge and Greater Boston. Submit a catering request or order directly through ezCater.
