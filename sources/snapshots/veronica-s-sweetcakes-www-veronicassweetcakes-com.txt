Veronica’s Sweetcakes Caffeine Game: Green Mountain and Nespresso
We proudly serve Green Mountain Coffee Roasters Fair Trade Organic Blends including Our House Blend, Decaf, Dark Magic, Caramel Vanilla Cream and Seasonal Flavors of Island Coconut, Pumpkin Spice and Wild Mountain Blueberry.
We serve Nespresso premium quality espresso drinks. We offer Ristretto and Decaffeinato.
Ristretto:
Pure and dark-roasted South and Central American Arabicas make Ristretto a coffee with a dense body and distinct cocoa notes.
Espresso Decaffeinato:
Dark roasted South American Arabicas with a touch of Robusta bring out the subtle cocoa and roasted cereal notes of this full-bodied decaffeinated espresso.
