Philosophy
Lindsay believes tarot is a tool to tap into your higher self for guidance. We often feel stuck in certain areas of our lives because we can’t tune into our intuition or how we
really
feel about something or someone. Tarot is a way to access those areas to gain clarity, and often validation. The cards can be revealing, but they always hold messages that support your healing and highest good. Her style is questions-based, so please come prepared with a question for your reading. The most popular topics for questions are love, money, career, and challenging relationships (romantic, platonic, or familial). Truthfully, you can ask the cards just about anything - except for questions about your health. And if you’re not sure what to ask? Lindsay is happy to help you feel into the question.
