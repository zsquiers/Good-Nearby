Welcome to Ward’s Berry Farm
Spread across 175 acres of land in Sharon, MA, Ward’s Berry Farm is a working farm and destination market rooted in local agriculture and seasonal experiences. Here you can spend the day picking fresh produce, enjoying hayrides, and gathering for lunch or ice cream.
In our farm store you’ll find produce grown on our farm and sourced from trusted parners, along with thoughtfully curated groceries, prepared foods, and homemade baked goods.
Pick-Your-Own activities run from April through November, and our farm store and sandwich counter are open year-round!
