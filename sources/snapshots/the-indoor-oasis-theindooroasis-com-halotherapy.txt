Halotherapy
What Is Halotherapy (Salt Therapy)?
Halotherapy is a salt based treatment that can alleviate a number of conditions. Halotherapy is a holistic, drug-free and natural therapy to help promote better breathing, stress relief, improved sleep, healthier skin, relief from respiratory conditions, and overall wellness.
For centuries, monks used naturally-occuring salt caverns to treat respiratory ailments and salt miners experienced lower rates or respiratory issues. The halotherapy room at The Indoor Oasis mimics this environment, enabling you to receive the same benefits.
Halotherapy is anti-microbial, which means that it is a substance that kills or slows the growth of micro-organisms, including bacteria, fungi, and viruses.
It is natural and safe and there are no side effects.
What To Expect
Our individual salt booth provides an enclosed environment where purified salt is broken down into tiny particles and dispersed through the air.
Halotherapy is a user-friendly, passive form of treatment; no action required. All you have to do is sit back and breathe in the salt-infused mist in a relaxing, safe tranquil environment.
Our individual salt room is fantastic for couples or for a single person if you just want to relax and catch up on some reading.
Benefits of Halotherapy
Will support you in keeping your immune system and lungs strong.
Having a strong immune system and lungs is an important part of everyone’s self care.
Provides relief for respiratory issues
Such as asthma, allergies, bronchitis, COPD, colds, cystic fibrosis, ear infections, and sinusitis all benefit from the use of Halotherapy.
Lower stress levels
Regulate sleep patterns, especially in combination with other therapies such as chiropractic care, nutritional guidance, and massage.
Can help clear the mind.

Frequently Asked Questions.
How is halotherapy at Indoor Oasis different than a traditional salt cave?
The Indoor Oasis VIP salt booth treatments take only 20 minutes while still providing the same benefits as an hour-long session in a traditional salt cave.
This makes it easier to fit a salt booth treatment into your busy schedule while also providing a more private and relaxing environment than a large salt cave.
We also offer relaxation music to complement your treatment.
How many session per week are recommended?
Halotherapy offers the best results when practiced regularly. The benefits are cumulative, so consistency and frequency are the key.
Those looking to incorporate Salt Therapy into their regular wellness routine would benefit from a weekly session.
People with chronic issues will benefit greatly if visiting salt rooms at least 2-4 times a week.
Halotherapy is safe to use on a daily basis if desired.
Will I be able to see or taste the salt?
You will not be able to see or smell the salt. You may, however, start to taste some salt on your lips.
Is it safe? What about for kids?
Yes, halotherapy is natural and drug-free. The salt comes from a natural source and is naturally processed to remove all of its impure elements without adding any fillers.
According to a 2017 Israeli study, halotherapy can also have “beneficial effects in mild asthmatic children.”
Halotherapy is also great for students and athletes because of its effects on cognition and athletic performance.
