At Redeye Roasters we seek to make people’s lives incrementally better by getting them to think differently about coffee. We serve an artisanal product that is carefully sourced, roasted and brewed to exacting standards. As coffee is an inherently social beverage, we have built strong relationships with farmers, suppliers, customers and the greater community. Our belief is that by sharing our passion for coffee we can elevate people’s expectations and make our coffee an everyday staple in their lives – something they can look forward to for both its deliciousness and the commitment it represents.
