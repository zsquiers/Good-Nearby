A Boutique Yoga + Movement Studio in Medway, MA
Drift + Oak is a boutique yoga + movement studio in Medway, Massachusetts offering yoga, mat pilates, mobility, barre, and functional fitness classes for all levels.
Our thoughtfully sequenced classes are designed to help you build strength, reduce stress, improve flexibility, and reconnect with yourself. Whether you're stepping onto the mat for the first time or have been practicing for years, you'll find a welcoming community and a space that feels like home.
From energizing vinyasa flows to grounding yin and restorative practices, every class is intentionally crafted to engage the body, calm the mind, and support your overall well-being.
At Drift + Oak, we believe movement is more than exercise. It is a way to breathe more deeply, move more freely, and show up more fully in your life.
the work in, not just the workout
Your practice becomes a dialogue with your body's deeper wisdom, where mindful movement creates space for physical tensions to dissolve. By honoring the body as a living archive, what's stored in muscle gradually unwinds, creating pathways for renewed vitality. As the outside world fades, you discover the transformation that emerges when truly listening to your body.
