About the Café
Angel’s Café was born out of Jeff’s love of coffee and his dream to create a haven where customers could sip, stroll, and stay a while as they perused premium plants, drank locally-roasted coffee, and munched on yummy treats.
Angel’s Café is praised for its quality coffee, unique baked goods, and friendly customer service. All baked goods are baked fresh daily and on site.
We offer seasonal specials to celebrate each time of the year. Subscribe to our newsletter and follow us on our socials to stay in-the-know about our seasonal offerings.

Red Barn Coffee
Red Barn Coffee consists of 100% Arabica coffee beans roasted locally in Upton, MA. Plus, it’s certified organic and Fair Trade.
Red Barn Coffee roasts various flavors right into their beans, which is why we can offer gluten-free and sugar-free brewed coffee in our café.
This grassroots company has helped pioneer the specialty coffee revolution in Massachusetts, and we are proud to serve this homegrown New England brand at Angel’s Café.
