Jamaica Pond parkrun
What is Jamaica Pond parkrun?
A free, fun, and friendly weekly 5k community event. Walk, jog, run, volunteer or spectate – it's up to you!
When is it?
Every Saturday at 9:00am.
Where is it?
The event takes place at Pinebank Promontory,
Jamaica Pond,
Boston, MA, 02130. See
Course page
for more details.
What does it cost to join in?
Nothing - it's free! but please
register
before you first come along. Only ever register with parkrun once and don't forget to bring a
scannable
copy of your barcode (
request a reminder
). If you forget it,
you won't get a time
.
How fast do I have to be?
We all take part for our own enjoyment. Please come along and join in whatever your pace!
Help Jamaica Pond parkrun happen!
All parkrun events are organised by volunteers - email
jamaicapond@parkrun.com
to help.

We're friendly!
Every week we grab a post parkrun coffee at Life Alive, 435 S Huntington Ave, Jamaica Plain, MA 02130 - please come and join us!
