AMC Boston Chapter
Of the AMC’s 12 chapters, Boston is the largest with over 20,000 members. The chapter is run 100% by volunteers who organize thousands of outings covering a wide range of activities throughout New England.

Appalachian Mountain Club
The mission of the Appalachian Mountain Club is to foster the protection, enjoyment, and understanding of the outdoors. We envision a world where our natural resources are healthy, loved, and always protected, and where the outdoors occupies a place of central importance in every person’s life. If you would like to join the AMC,
you can do that here!
Join us as we explore the outdoors!
We offer many programs and adventures throughout Massachusetts and New England. Whatever your interest is and whoever your people are, we have you covered.
