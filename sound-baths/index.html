Sound baths near you

A sound bath is about as low-effort as self-care gets: you lie down, close your eyes and let singing bowls, gongs or gentle music wash over you. Most are an hour or so, no experience is needed, and many hosts bring mats and blankets. Some are paired with yoga, acupuncture or a candlelit room; a few are free at local libraries.
