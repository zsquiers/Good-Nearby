Feel-good things to do in MetroWest

MetroWest runs west of Boston along Route 9 and the Mass Pike, from Wellesley and Needham out through Natick, Framingham and Sudbury. Natick's Common Street Spiritual Center holds yoga, meditation and sound sessions most days of the week. The Cochituate Rail Trail and Mass Audubon's Broadmoor Wildlife Sanctuary are close by when you'd rather walk, and local libraries host free craft classes and book groups.
