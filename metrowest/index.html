Feel-good things to do in MetroWest

MetroWest stretches west of Boston along the Route 9 and Mass Pike corridor, from Wellesley and Needham out through Natick, Framingham and Sudbury. It's rich in quiet places to slow down: spiritual centers with weekly yoga and meditation, rail trails around Lake Cochituate, Mass Audubon sanctuaries and well-loved local libraries with free classes and book groups.
