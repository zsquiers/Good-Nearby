Feel-good things to do in Natick, MA

Natick's Common Street Spiritual Center has something on nearly every day: gentle yoga, meditation groups, tai chi, dance and a monthly community sound bath. Outside, there's the Cochituate Rail Trail and Mass Audubon's Broadmoor Wildlife Sanctuary, with Charles River Coffee House for afterward.
