Franklin & nearby

Feel-good things to do in Franklin, MA

Downtown Franklin keeps several good things within a few blocks of each other: a bakery, an acupuncture studio and holistic boutique on East Central Street, and a yoga studio out on West Central. The SNETT Rail Trail and DelCarte's ponds are a few minutes away when you want to be outside.

Franklin has 16 good things listed on Good Nearby right now, including meditation, reiki & massage and trails. Regulars include Birchwood Bakery & Kitchen, Whimsical Wellness Boutique and SNETT Rail Trail.
