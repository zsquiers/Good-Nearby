Privacy

Last updated October 28, 2026

The short version: no accounts, no cookies, no ads, and we never sell anything about you. What you save stays on your own phone or computer. If you sign up for the newsletter, we keep your email address only to send it.

What stays on your device

These are saved in your browser only. We never see them:

  • Things you save with ♡ Save.
  • Your town, ZIP code or location, if you tell us "Where are you?" We use it on your device to show what's closest. "Use my location" asks your permission first, and keeps only a rough spot (rounded to about half a mile).
  • The area you last browsed.

To erase them, clear this site's data in your browser settings. "Text my saved list to myself" creates a link with your saved listings in it. It's only shared with whoever you send it to.

Weekend Favorites newsletter

If you sign up, your email address is stored with Buttondown, the service that sends the newsletter. We use it only to send Weekend Favorites. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing stops them right away. Buttondown may count opens and clicks to show us what people enjoyed.

"Share something good" form

If you send us a listing or a recommendation, what you type is delivered to us by email through Formspree. We use your name and email only to follow up about that listing. They're never published.

Visitor counts

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly counter, to see roughly how many people visit, which screens they view and which host links they tap. It uses no cookies and doesn't store your internet address or anything that identifies you. It does record general details like your browser, screen size, country and the site that sent you. Your saved list and location are never sent.

Other sites the page loads from

Like most websites, the page loads fonts from Google Fonts and photos from Unsplash and Pexels. Those services can see your device's internet address when they send the file, as with any website you visit. Links to hosts' pages, maps and sign-ups take you to their sites, which have their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

  • No cookies, no advertising, and no tracking across other websites.
  • No selling or sharing your information.
  • Good Nearby is meant for adults and isn't directed at children under 13.

About the listings

Listings are gathered from hosts' own pages, library and venue calendars, Eventbrite, Meetup and people like you. We don't run these events and can't guarantee them. Details can change, so please check with the host before you go. Nothing here is medical advice. If you have a health condition or are pregnant, check with your doctor before trying something new.

Questions or requests

Email findgoodnearby@gmail.com, for example to have your email removed or to ask what we hold about you. If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top.
